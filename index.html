About me

I help people learn digital marketing and help businesses grow online.

I'm Elankathir, a digital marketing freelancer and trainer passionate about helping businesses, students, and aspiring freelancers understand the digital world. Before this, I spent six years as a Customer Service Executive at Best Digital Communication in Perundurai, Erode — time that taught me how to listen closely to what people actually need.

Today I work across SEO, social media marketing, content marketing, keyword research, website development, and digital marketing strategy. Alongside freelancing, I provide affordable and practical courses designed for beginners who want to build real-world digital skills and explore career opportunities in the digital industry.
